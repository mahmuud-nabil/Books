# Chapter 1: Data Engineering Described

*Fundamentals of Data Engineering (Reis & Housley)*

## Key ideas

- Data engineering takes raw data and produces high-quality, consistent information for analytics, dashboards, and ML.
- Lifecycle: generation, storage, ingestion, transformation, serving. Storage underpins all the other stages.
- Six undercurrents cut across every stage: security, data management, DataOps, data architecture, orchestration, software engineering.
- Data maturity: starting with data, scaling with data, leading with data.
